About Us

Who We Are

AWS Student Builder Group at Charotar University of Science and Technology is a student-led, student-driven user group based at Changa, Gujarat. An ASC Club initiative dedicated to learning AWS Cloud technology together, we guide students through practical use cases in cloud infrastructure, security, AI, business analytics, and business transformation.

💻

Hands-on Lab Sessions

We prioritize lab time over purely theoretical slides. We walk through creating compute instances, setting up storage, and configuring basic networks together in campus labs.

🗺️

Curated Peer Roadmaps

While CHARUSAT's official AWS Academy covers formal curriculum, our club shares student-tested roadmaps, domain notes, and sample architecture questions to help peers prepare.

🛡️

Safe & Cost-Aware Learning

The biggest fear when starting cloud is getting a surprise bill. In every onboarding session, we teach students how to configure zero-cost billing alerts and stay strictly inside Free Tier limits.

>_ Terminal Basics

Getting comfortable with Linux commands, SSH keys, and the AWS Command Line Interface (CLI).

⚙️ Infrastructure as Code

Introducing Terraform so students can see how modern teams define and launch servers cleanly.

🤝 Peer Debugging

When code or configurations fail in the lab, seniors and team members sit with you to solve it.

💡 Industry Perspectives

Inviting experienced architects and mentors to share how these systems work in production.
